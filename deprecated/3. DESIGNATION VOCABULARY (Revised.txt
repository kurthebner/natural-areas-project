 3. DESIGNATION VOCABULARY (Revised v3.1)
Designation expresses legal, formal, or officially documented status applied to a Site.
It does not express identity (Category) or function (Subtype).
Designations are grouped by jurisdiction.

A. Federal Designations (Revised)
National Park
National Monument
National Historic Site
National Memorial
National Historic Landmark
National Natural Landmark
National Recreation Area
National Wildlife Refuge
National Scenic Trail
National Heritage Area
National Battlefield
National Cemetery
National Register of Historic Places (NRHP)
Notes
- These apply only when the Site itself is formally designated.
- NRHP is a designation, not a Category.
- National Scenic Trail applies only when the Site is the trail corridor, not when a trail passes through a Site.
- National Wildlife Refuge is a governance designation, not a Category.

D. Blank (No Designation)
Most Sites have no designation.
This is a valid and common state.

Designation Rules (Revised)
- Use only when explicitly stated in authoritative sources.
- Do not infer designation from context, appearance, or management.
- Do not combine multiple designations unless explicitly documented.
- Designation must not duplicate Category or Subtype.
- Designation must not encode temporary conditions.
- Designation must not be used for marketing names or informal labels.
- If a Site has multiple official designations, list them separated by semicolons in alphabetical order.
- Designation must apply to the Site, not to a network, trail, or institution.
