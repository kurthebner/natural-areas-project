### Finding

Wood County Government does **NOT** directly manage any parks, trails, preserves, or natural areas separate from the Wood County Park District (Tier 3).

**Key Evidence:**
- Wood County Park District (established 1934) is a separate governmental entity that handles ALL county-level park and recreation management
- County Commissioners oversee 12 departments: Building Inspection, Job/Family Services, Economic Development, Wood Haven Health Care, Buildings & Grounds, Dog Shelter, Emergency Management, Historical Museum, IT, Records Center, Solid Waste Management
- **NO recreation or parks department exists** at county level
- Adam Phillips Pond is co-managed (County Commissioners + Park District), already counted in Tier 3
- Soil & Water Conservation District focuses on agricultural conservation, not public recreation

**Conclusion:** Common Ohio pattern - counties delegate all park/recreation management to separate Park Districts rather than managing facilities directly.
