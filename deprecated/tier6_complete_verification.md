## LESSONS LEARNED

### **Critical Findings:**

1. **Never assume official pages are complete**
   - Weston had 3 parks listed, actually has 4
   - Elementary Park visible on maps but not on website

2. **Small villages often have parks**
   - Portage (418 pop): 1 park
   - Jerry City (454 pop): 1 park
   - Custar (178 pop): 1+ parks
   - Bairdstown (115 pop): 1+ parks

3. **Multiple verification methods essential**
   - Historical markers work (Wayne)
   - Financial documents work (Bairdstown)
   - Grant records work (Portage, Custar)
   - User knowledge works (Jerry City, Custar, Weston)

4. **Web searches alone miss parks**
   - 6 villages showed "0 parks" in searches but had parks
   - Map viewing is mandatory (per methodology v5.0)
