CATEGORY DEFINITIONS 
1. Park
A land unit designated for public recreation, leisure, or enjoyment, typically containing amenities, trails, open areas, or programmed spaces.
- Identity is recreational
- May include facilities, greenspaces, or natural areas
- Not primarily ecological or conservation‑driven
Subtypes of Park
Greenspace
A minimally developed, vegetated area designated as part of a park or civic open‑space system.
- May be named or unnamed
- Lacks significant built amenities
- Managed for passive use, aesthetics, or buffering

2. Natural Area
A natural or semi‑natural land unit managed for ecological value, passive recreation, or low‑impact use, without legal protection or specialized wildlife management.
- Not curated
- Not legally protected
- Not programmed like a park
- Functions as the “default” identity for natural land

3. Nature Preserve
A legally protected land unit dedicated to conservation of natural features, species, or ecosystems.
- Highest ecological protection
- Restricted or regulated access
- Managed for conservation priority

4. Wildlife Area
A land unit managed primarily for wildlife habitat, hunting, fishing, or wildlife‑related recreation.
- Often state‑managed
- May include regulated hunting/fishing
- Habitat‑focused

5. Conservation Area
A land unit managed for ecological stewardship, restoration, or resource protection, without the legal status of a Nature Preserve.
- Restoration, stewardship, or resource‑focused
- May allow limited recreation
- Not legally protected

6. Open Space
A designated land unit preserved for its openness, character, or civic value, typically within urban or suburban contexts.
- May be vegetated or partially developed
- Identity is civic, not recreational
- Not necessarily natural

7. Recreation Facility
A land unit whose primary identity is built recreational infrastructure.
- Sports complexes
- Skate parks
- Pools
- Recreation centers
- Athletic fields

8. Cultural Facility
A land unit whose identity is tied to cultural, artistic, or interpretive functions.
- Cultural centers
- Performing arts complexes
- Interpretive centers
- Heritage facilities

9. Historic Site
A land unit whose identity is defined by historical significance, events, structures, or interpretation.
- May include buildings or landscapes
- History is the defining identity

10. Cemetery
A land unit dedicated to burial, memorialization, and funerary functions.
- May include natural or landscaped areas
- Identity is commemorative and functional

11. Memorial
A land unit dedicated to commemoration of individuals, groups, or events.
- Not a cemetery
- May be landscaped or built
- Identity is symbolic

12. Community Garden
A land unit dedicated to community‑managed horticulture, typically plot‑based.
- Civic and participatory
- Not agricultural or commercial

13. Campground
A land unit designated for overnight outdoor accommodation.
- May include tent sites, RV sites, cabins
- Recreation‑oriented

14. Water Site
A land unit whose identity is defined by a water body or water‑centric function.
- Lakes
- Ponds
- Reservoirs
- Harbors
- Water access areas

15. Curated Biological Site
A land unit whose identity is defined by a curated biological collection of living organisms.
- Plants, animals, insects, aquatic life
- Mission‑driven (education, conservation, research, display)
- Indoor, outdoor, or mixed
Subtypes of Curated Biological Site
Arboretum
A curated collection of trees and woody plants arranged for education, research, conservation, or display.
Botanical Garden
A curated collection of plants arranged for education, conservation, research, or display.
Zoo
A curated collection of terrestrial animals maintained for conservation, education, research, or display.
Aquarium
A curated collection of aquatic animals and ecosystems.
Aviary
A curated collection of birds.
Insectarium
A curated collection of insects and arthropods.
Butterfly House
A curated collection of butterflies and moths in a controlled environment.
Reptile House
A curated collection of reptiles and amphibians.
Biopark (optional)
A mixed‑taxa curated biological site integrating plants and animals.
Living Museum (optional)
A curated biological site with strong interpretive or cultural integration.

16. Museum
A land unit dedicated to the collection, preservation, and interpretation of cultural, historical, artistic, or scientific materials.
- Distinct from Cultural Facility when the museum itself is the land unit

17. Hunting Area
A land unit designated for public hunting access and wildlife‑related recreation.
- Governance‑defined
- Identity‑bearing
- Mapped and managed

18. Fishing Area
A land unit designated for public fishing access, often including shoreline, piers, or water‑adjacent parcels.
- Governance‑defined
- Identity‑bearing
- Distinct from Water Site when the fishing function is primary
