## About This Document

This tracker captures every protocol gap, vocabulary gap, discovery method issue, and architectural question surfaced during active county work. It is the authoritative record of what we noticed, what we decided, and what still needs to be done.

County-specific data issues (deduplication flags, coverage gaps, partial records) belong in the relevant county's handoff document — not here.

**How to use it:**
- When a new issue is discovered during any county session, add it to **Section 1 (Open)** immediately.
- After analysis and a decision, move it to **Section 2 (Decided — Pending Implementation)** with the decision recorded.
- After the relevant protocol or vocabulary document is updated, move it to **Section 3 (Decided & Implemented)**.

**Item ID format:** `IMP-###` (sequential, project-wide).
