## About This Document

This tracker captures every protocol gap, vocabulary gap, discovery method issue,
and architectural question surfaced during v6.x development and active county work.
It is the authoritative record of what we noticed, what we decided, and what still
needs to be done.

County-specific data issues (deduplication flags, coverage gaps, partial records)
belong in the relevant county's handoff document — not here.

**Relationship to v5 tracker**: The v5 improvement tracker (`na_improvement_tracker.md`
in the v5 project folder) remains the authoritative record for all IMP-001 through
IMP-135 items. This tracker covers v6.x work only. IMP numbers here are v6-scoped
and begin at IMP-001; they do not collide with v5 IMP numbers.

**How to use it:**
- When a new issue is discovered during any county session or module review, add it
  to **Section 1 (Open)** immediately.
- After analysis and a decision, move it to **Section 2 (Decided — Pending
  Implementation)** with the decision recorded.
- After the relevant protocol or vocabulary document is updated, move it to
  **Section 3 (Decided & Implemented)**.

**Item ID format:** `IMP-###` (sequential, v6-scoped).
