These three entity types were not evaluated during original discovery. Evaluated during pipeline review 2026-04-14. Results are confirmed nulls.

### Trail Segments — NULL ✅
Evaluated for all 3 Wood County trails:

- **Portage River Water Trail**: Ohio water trails use Access Points for river entry/exit, not Trail Segment entities. No named segments in ODNR records.
- **Maumee River Water Trail**: Same as above. WOD-AP-001/002/003 capture Wood County access points.

All tiers confirmed null for trail segments. Sources checked: wcparks.org, ODNR Coastal Management, wildohio.gov.

### Trail Networks — NULL ✅
Key research question: Is "Maumee River Trail" referenced in WCPD Buttonwood and Otsego Park records a distinct land-based trail or trail network?

Fetched wcparks.org: Only the Maumee River Water Trail (paddling) is formally named. "Maumee River Trail access" in WCPD park descriptions is informal language for river-adjacent access, not a formal trail network entity. No WCPD trail system connects multiple preserves under a unified network identity.

All tiers confirmed null for trail networks. Sources checked: wcparks.org (trails section), Metroparks Toledo, NORTA, ODNR Coastal Management.
